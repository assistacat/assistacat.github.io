SEARCH→RANK→TEST

BT4222 · TEAM COURSEWORK

Personalising online product search

When two shoppers search for the same thing, should they see the same results? Our team explored how an online store could reorder its search results for each shopper. We compared approaches that used the search terms, product details, and the shopper’s earlier clicks and purchases to decide which products should appear first.

My contribution. I improved how we used shoppers’ past activity and reran the evaluation on 20,000 shoppers. I also compared a simpler model with a more complex one using the same information. Both performed similarly at putting relevant products in the top ten.
